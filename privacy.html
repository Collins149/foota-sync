Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Football LiveScoreStream ("the app") shows football fixtures, results and match information. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

The app does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or precise location, and we do not collect personal information ourselves.

Services the app uses

The app uses Google Firebase services to work:

  • Cloud Firestore delivers match and league data to the app. When you open a match, the app adds one to that match's view counter. The counter is not linked to you.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers notifications. For this, Google assigns your device an identifier. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings at any time.

These services may process technical data such as your IP address and device identifiers. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Data on your device

The app stores simple settings on your device, such as whether you have completed the welcome screens. This information stays on your device.

Video

When a match includes a video stream, the video is loaded from the address provided for that match. The company hosting the video may receive technical data such as your IP address.

Data sources

Football data is provided by the Football-Data.org API. Scores and schedules may be delayed. Team and league names and logos belong to their respective owners.

Security

We care about protecting your information. The app does not hold personal accounts, and the data it uses is stored with Google Firebase, which applies its own security measures. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

The app does not address anyone under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us at the address below so we can take the necessary action, including deleting that information.

Your consent
