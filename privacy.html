Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Football LiveScoreStream ("the app") shows football fixtures, results and match information. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Information we collect

The app does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location. We do not collect personal information ourselves.

Services the app uses

The app uses Google Firebase services to work:

  • Cloud Firestore delivers match, league and view-count data to the app. When you open a match, the app adds one to that match's view counter. The counter is not linked to you.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers notifications. For this, Google assigns your device an identifier. You can turn notifications off in your phone settings at any time.

These services may process technical data such as your IP address and device identifiers. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Data on your device

The app stores simple settings on your device, such as whether you have completed the welcome screens. This stays on your device.

Video

When a match includes a video stream, the video is loaded from the address provided for that match. The company hosting the video may receive technical data such as your IP address.

Data sources

Football data is provided by the Football-Data.org API. Team and league names and logos belong to their respective owners.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
